# Yanbo (Fred) Jing

Ph.D. Student in Chemical Biology at **Peking University**.

My research lies at the intersection of **protein design,
chemical biology, and artificial intelligence**. I am particularly
interested in developing computational approaches for de novo protein
design and expanding the chemical space of proteins through
noncanonical amino acids (ncAAs).

My early research focused on bioorthogonal reactions, click chemistry, and
biocompatible residue chemistry. My computational background also includes
density functional theory (DFT), molecular dynamics (MD), and molecular docking.
These experiences inform my current work on protein design and chemical biology.

### Research Interests

- **Protein Design** — De novo design, structure prediction, and
  protein representation learning.
- **Chemical Biology** — Bioorthogonal reactions, click chemistry,
  biocompatible residue chemistry, genetic code expansion, and engineering
  proteins with noncanonical amino acids.
- **AI for Science** — Deep learning for biomolecular modeling,
  design, and experimental discovery.
